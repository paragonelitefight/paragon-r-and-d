# Paragon R&D / From design intent to physical evidence

Public documentation date: 19 November 2026. This is a presentation update, not a new engineering revision or test report.

## What readers can inspect

The programme presents two equipment concepts: PARAGON CÆSTUS ZERO, a professional training-glove project, and PARAGON UNTOUCHABLE NØ-G, a hybrid BJJ gi project. Both are documented development work rather than validated retail products. The value of publishing the documents is to make the intended construction, unresolved decisions and proposed validation path inspectable. Publication does not establish that a prototype has been manufactured or tested.

## The glove investigation

The working design combines purpose-zoned leather, an experimental layered padding architecture, an anatomical hand chamber, a rounded striking crown and directional wrist-support geometry. The useful engineering questions concern material sourcing, actual construction, fit, impact response, fatigue and repeatability, not whether the presentation looks futuristic.

Auxetic and rate-sensitive materials are design selections. A cited material finding is not a measured result for an assembled glove. Statements inside the original working specification about uniqueness, injury prevention or superiority are design-author assertions or ambitions, not outcomes independently established by this documentation release.

The stated 36 mm padding total and 38 mm sum of listed layers remain an explicit development clarification. This presentation update does not resolve the discrepancy, change a layer or approve a manufacturing tolerance.

## The garment investigation

The gi explores different fabrics and construction roles across a garment: grip zones, articulated movement areas, reinforcement, collar structure and skin-contact regions. The published README maps these choices to the original tech pack. Mobility, abrasion behaviour, shrinkage, comfort and fit require assessment on actual samples under recorded conditions.

Material brands, named standards and supplier options are not certifications or evidence of commercial partnerships. Proposed copper-containing yarns do not establish antimicrobial or infection-prevention performance in the finished garment. A proposed NFC component does not establish a deployed production traceability service.

## An evidence-led development sequence

1. Identify the exact document revision and resolve construction ambiguities before treating them as fixed production instructions.
2. Record selected materials and any substitutions against the intended function.
3. Build and identify samples; record deviations from the working documents.
4. Record test methods, conditions, sample identifiers and results, including failures and revisions.
5. Separate physical testing, athlete feedback, competition requirements and production decisions. None substitutes automatically for the others.

This is a proposed review sequence, not a report that these steps have been completed. No new physical test, comparative trial or supplier approval is asserted here.

# Paragon R&D

### Advanced fight equipment. Documented development.

Paragon Elite Fight Group's research and product-development programme for boxing gloves and Brazilian jiu-jitsu equipment.

We are developing two equipment concepts with a shared ambition: to pursue exceptional performance through explicit design requirements, prototype iteration and meaningful testing.

This repository documents that development journey. It is not a catalogue of finished products.

**Current stage: research, engineering specification and prototype development.**

## Two projects. One evidence-led approach.

### Professional Sparring & Bag Glove

A training-glove development project exploring the relationship between hand fit, impact attenuation, wrist movement and serviceability.

The detailed working specification and companion workshop drawings describe:

- Anatomical glove shape and attached-thumb geometry.
- A proposed auxetic-foam padding system with an internal load-spreading element.
- Directional wrist-support construction.
- Internal lacing beneath a protective closure flap.
- Ventilation and repair-oriented construction.
- Planned impact, fatigue and pressure-distribution testing.

These are design proposals and validation objectives. Findings from material studies are not presented as measured performance of this glove.

Intended use: training, not competition approval.

### PROJECT KOMA / KMA-01

A hybrid, zoned BJJ gi development project that assigns different material and construction requirements to different garment regions.

Revision A documents:

- Articulated patterning for grappling positions.
- Zoned fabrics for structure, mobility, ventilation and wear resistance.
- Detailed collar, cuff and waistband construction.
- Size grading and prototype-development stages.
- Planned laboratory, fit and athlete wear testing.

PROJECT KOMA is a working name. The current specification intentionally targets training and open-rules use. It is not presented as an IBJJF-approved uniform.

## From specification to evidence

Our development pathway:

**Design requirements → Prototype → Measurement → Revision → Further validation → Production decision**

Targets may change during prototyping. Material substitutions, dimensional adjustments and design changes should be recorded in subsequent revisions.

Test plans are not test results. Naming a test standard or material brand does not establish certification, supplier approval or a commercial partnership.

Prototype completion, measured performance and production readiness will be reported when supported by the relevant records.
